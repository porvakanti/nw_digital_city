The ground
A district is an L2 area, inside its own coloured kerb. A plot is an L3 group, the paler slab inside it. A lot is one L4 category, one square of land. Bigger districts hold more categories, though the packing leaves gaps, so read that roughly rather than exactly. Everything green is parkland between the districts and means nothing.
